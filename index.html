About

Standub. A one-man-band performance. From dreamy soundscapes to heavy basslines, Standub explores a broad musical spectrum that gets your body moving.

Beatboxing, vocals, and synthesis — everything happens live, on the spot.

A musical experience that refuses to be pinned down to a single genre, emerging entirely through original creation and improvisation.
